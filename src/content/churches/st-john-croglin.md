  what: A village group approached the Architectural Heritage Fund and received £8,500 for a viability study, then £325 for an asbestos survey. The Diocese of Carlisle had planned to sell for a house and agreed a provisional 125-year lease at a peppercorn rent instead.

  what: The group became a charity, The Helm at Croglin. Its ideas included the village post office, a heritage centre for the fellside and overnight stays in the church.
- when: October 2025
  what: The charity could not raise the money. The diocese would not wait, and PFK listed the church at £100,000 for cash buyers.

The building is tidy and dry, with a clock, and the village has a vampire legend of its own. Planning advice says the church may be converted to a house. The guide price is £100,000, for a cash buyer.

The work is routine: a drain connection, new heating and asbestos removal. A village charity hoped to make it a community hub, so a buyer who keeps the village involved would start well.
