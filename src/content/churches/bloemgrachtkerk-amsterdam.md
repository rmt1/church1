story:

  what: A group that has broken from the state church begins to meet in a galleried room in the house of its minister, Simon van Velsen, a few doors along the canal.
- when: April 1880
  what: The building permit is granted. Two warehouses at number 98 come down.
- when: December 1880
  what: The church is finished for the Christelijk Gereformeerde congregation. No architect is recorded. The newspaper De Standaard reports that members of the church council supervised the work.

  what: P.J. Adema builds the organ.
- when: '1927'
  what: The first congregation has moved away and the Hersteld Apostolische Zendingkerk takes the building. It reworks the wall behind the pulpit in the Amsterdam School manner.

  what: The municipality orders the turret taken down because its anchors have rusted through.
- when: February 2020
  what: Nedstede announces that it has bought the church and will restore the turret.

---

This is a church held as stock by a property company. The price is set by the Jordaan, and the building work starts with a turret that has been missing for seven years.

A buyer who keeps the galleries and the organ would be doing what the local heritage society has asked for in print.
