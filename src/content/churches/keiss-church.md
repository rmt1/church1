---

This is one of the churches Parliament paid for in the 1820s to serve remote Highland parishes, built to a single design by Thomas Telford. Keiss has kept the features that mark the type: the cast-iron tracery, the bellcote and the plain interior.

It is small, harled and connected to water, power and the sewer. Nothing urgent shows in the photographs, and it is among the simplest churches on the shortlist to take on.
