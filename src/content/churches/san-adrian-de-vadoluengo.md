story:
- when: '1035'
  what: Sancho the Great gives his son Ramiro the lands that become Aragon. The ford at Vadoluengo is named as a boundary.
- when: About 1122
  what: Alfonso the Battler grants the place to Fortún Garcés Cajal, one of his lords, who builds a palace and this church.
- when: '1133'
  what: Fortún and his wife Toda give the church of San Adrián and the estate to the Cluniac monastery of Nájera, keeping it for their lifetimes.
- when: January 1135
  what: García of Navarre and Ramiro II of Aragon agree the Pact of Vadoluengo here, with Fortún among the negotiators. It lasts about five months.
- when: '1141'
  what: Bishop Sancho de Larrosa of Pamplona consecrates the church. Fortún and Toda give it to the abbey of Cluny, and monks move in the same day. It becomes the first Cluniac priory in Navarre.
- when: '1145'
  what: After Toda dies, Fortún repeats the gift before several abbots to put it beyond doubt.
- when: 13th century
  what: Benedictine monks who left Leyre when the Cistercians arrived are housed here.
- when: '1312'
  what: Navarre and Aragon fight the battle of Vadoluengo at the ford.
- when: Later centuries
  what: The priory ends and the building is used as a farmhouse and granary.

  what: Declared a historic-artistic monument.

The advert is careless about facts that matter. Confirm the monument status, the services and the exact boundaries with the agent before anything else.
