This is the kind of church the site exists for. It is small enough that one person can pay for the roof, old enough to repay the attention, and it has been private property for more than two centuries.

Spend the first two years on water: the north slope, the wall plate, the gutters. Leave the glass and the floor until the building is dry. Ask the agent about the bell and the statue before you visit.
