A church has stood at Torthorwald since the 13th century, and the present kirk of 1782 keeps two carved stones from its predecessors in the vestry wall. It looks over Dumfries to the Solway.

The inside is clean and still set up for worship, with its organ of 1904. The walls outside need their limewash renewed and the heating needs replacing.
