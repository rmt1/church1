- when: 1658 to 1678
  what: Slabs for Joyce Walker, Maria Hare, William Walker and Jane Hare. All but one carry a coat of arms cast in relief.

  what: Slabs for Jane and Barbara Knight, Richard Knight, who died in 1745, and Ralph Knight. Richard Knight ran Bringewood and built one of the great iron fortunes of the 18th century. The listing says the slabs were probably cast at the family's own works.

It is a small, complete church in good country. Below the roof, everything looks sound in the photographs.

The roof is the job. It is shedding tiles and has been declared dangerous, so it is priced as a full replacement. With the roof back on, this is a dry church with a group of iron grave slabs that the official listing calls unusual.
