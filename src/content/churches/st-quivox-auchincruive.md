Sixty thousand pounds for eight hundred years of parish church with a 1767 interior. It has water, power and a drain, which most of the Kirk's country churches do not.

The closing date has gone. Ring the Law Department first. If it is still free, pay for a roof survey before you offer, and plan a use that leaves the pews alone.
