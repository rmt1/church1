The interior is the reason to take this church on. Three panelled galleries, built for three landed families, face a pulpit of 1767 across pine pews, and nothing in the photographs has been altered.

The church closed in September 2025. The walls and ceiling are clean and the roof was repaired in 1985. The heating needs replacing, and a survey is the first step.
