---

This is the largest of the Scottish churches on the shortlist and the most ambitious in its architecture: a red sandstone church with a 140-foot spire, standing above the Gala Water beside the old packhorse bridge.

The interior is complete, with its timber roof, its glass and its organ, and Stow has a station on the line to Edinburgh. The spire and the roof are where the cost lies, so an inspection of both comes first.
