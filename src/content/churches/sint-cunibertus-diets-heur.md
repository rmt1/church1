  what: The stone is recut as a holy water stoup and a mask head is added. A flint plinth in the north wall may survive from a Romanesque church of this time.

  what: The village is first recorded, as Eure. Its chapel of St Cunibert answers to the parish of Vreren.

The starting price is the lowest on the shortlist outside Sicily. The cost is in the energy rules, which treat a church of 1850 like a house with a poor label.

The organ from Maredsous and the Roman font are the reasons to care about this one. Get both written into the sale.
