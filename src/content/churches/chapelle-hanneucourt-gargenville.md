story:

- when: '1727'
  what: Moufle de la Tuilerie builds the present château, with an orangery, a dovecote, stables and a private chapel.

  what: Napoleon I visits.

  what: The château is remodelled. Its visitors include Casimir Périer and Mademoiselle Dosne, the sister-in-law of Adolphe Thiers. The château's address today is 1 avenue Thiers.
- when: 20th century
  what: The château becomes a reception centre and is recorded as a social-care building. A retirement home is proposed.

  what: Part of the estate is inscrit as a monument historique. The order names the south front and its roof, the chapel, the dovecote, the orangery and the fountain.

- when: Today
  what: The outbuildings are being converted, and the chapel is offered for sale on its own.

This chapel never belonged to the Church. It was built for the owners of a château, and it is being sold on its own now that the estate is being divided.

The checkerboard floor, the panelling, the painted decoration and the bell are all still there after years of neglect. Forty kilometres from Paris, that is rare. Find out exactly how much ground comes with it and whether the statues stay.
